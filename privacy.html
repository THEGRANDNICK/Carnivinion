Carnivinion – Privacy Policy

Short version: Carnivinion collects no personal data. Everything stays on your device.

What is stored

Your settings, collection, history, player names, results, your own cards and any pictures or rulebooks you import are stored only in your browser/app storage on your device (localStorage and IndexedDB). Nothing is sent to a server operated by the author. There are no accounts, no tracking, no analytics and no ads.

Camera

The camera is only used when you tap “Scan” or “Take photo”, to read a QR code or to photograph your own cards. Images are processed on your device and are not uploaded.

Card recognition

“Scan the table” reads card names from your photo with text recognition that runs entirely on your device (Tesseract). The photo is not uploaded. The recognition files are downloaded once from jsDelivr.

Sync with friends (optional)

If you connect a GitHub repository in the settings, your results and player names are stored in a file in your own repository via the GitHub API, using the token you entered. The token is kept only on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere if you don't set this up.

Third-party services

To display the app, fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and small open-source libraries (JSZip, qrcodejs, jsQR, Tesseract.js) from public CDNs (cdnjs, jsDelivr). These providers may see your IP address as part of a normal web request. The app is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Sharing

When you share a kingdom, the link or QR code only contains the card selection – no personal data.

Deleting your data

Delete the app or clear the site data in your browser to remove everything.

Datenschutz (Kurzfassung)

Carnivinion erhebt keine personenbezogenen Daten. Alle Einstellungen, Namen, Ergebnisse, Bilder und Regelhefte bleiben nur auf deinem Gerät. Die Kamera wird nur zum Scannen von QR-Codes und zum Fotografieren eigener Karten genutzt; die Bilder verlassen das Gerät nicht. Die Kartenerkennung („Tisch scannen“) läuft vollständig auf deinem Gerät; das Foto wird nicht hochgeladen. Schriften und Bibliotheken werden von Google Fonts, cdnjs und jsDelivr geladen; dabei kann deine IP-Adresse übertragen werden. Optional kannst du Ergebnisse über dein eigenes GitHub-Repository mit Freunden synchronisieren; der Token bleibt auf deinem Gerät. Keine Werbung, kein Tracking, keine Konten.
